# Phishing Email Analysis Report

## 1. Objective

The objective of this task is to analyze a suspicious email sample and
identify common phishing characteristics such as sender spoofing,
suspicious links, urgency, threats, and social engineering techniques.

## 2. Email Sample

The analyzed email claims to be from PayPal Security and asks the
recipient to verify their account because of unusual activity.

## 3. Phishing Indicators Identified

### 3.1 Suspicious Sender Address

The email claims to be from PayPal Security, but the sender uses the
domain `example.com` instead of an official PayPal domain. This mismatch
is a strong warning sign.

### 3.2 Urgent Subject

The subject contains the word "URGENT" and claims that the PayPal account
has been limited. This is intended to create panic and encourage quick
action.

### 3.3 Threatening Language

The email states that the account may be permanently suspended if the
recipient does not act within 24 hours. This creates pressure and fear.

### 3.4 Suspicious URL

The email contains the URL:

`http://paypal-security-verification.example.com/login`

The domain does not match the legitimate PayPal domain. The URL should
not be clicked.

### 3.5 Generic Greeting

The email uses "Dear Customer" instead of identifying the recipient by
name. Generic greetings can be a warning sign in phishing emails.

### 3.6 Social Engineering

The message uses fear, urgency, and the possibility of account
suspension to influence the recipient into taking immediate action.

## 4. Header Analysis

The provided sample is a simulated text email and does not contain
original email headers such as Received, Return-Path, SPF, DKIM, or
Authentication-Results.

Therefore, genuine header analysis cannot be performed on this sample.

For a real phishing investigation, the original email headers should be
obtained and analyzed using a trusted email-header analysis tool.

## 5. Risk Assessment

The email should be considered suspicious because it contains multiple
phishing indicators:

- Suspicious sender domain
- Urgent language
- Threat of account suspension
- Suspicious verification link
- Generic greeting
- Social engineering techniques

## 6. Recommended Actions

A user receiving a suspicious email should:

1. Avoid clicking links.
2. Avoid opening unknown attachments.
3. Verify the sender through an independent method.
4. Visit the organization's official website directly instead of using
   links in the email.
5. Report the suspicious email to the appropriate security team or email
   provider.
6. Delete the message after reporting it, if appropriate.

## 7. Conclusion

The analyzed sample demonstrates several common phishing techniques.
The attacker attempts to impersonate a trusted organization and uses
urgency, fear, and a suspicious verification link to influence the
recipient.

This analysis demonstrates the importance of checking the sender,
links, language, and email headers before taking action on suspicious
emails.
